# Live test, 2026-10-28: Milestone 1 on real Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby forms

Twelve live, public Postings, filled by the built extension with the real helper running the real `claude` (2.1.311) and `codex` (0.158.0) on this Mac. The persona is made up. Nothing was submitted, no CAPTCHA was touched, no account was created, and nothing on a job site was clicked by the test: only the Fill wrote to the forms, through `act`.

**Result: every field ended filled, needs review or couldn't fill. Four fields were silently wrong in the first passes; all four are fixed, and the fixes were re-checked on the live forms. Silently wrong after the fixes: 0.**

## How it ran

- **Harness:** `pnpm test:live` (`e2e/live/`, `playwright.live.config.ts`). It never runs in CI or `pnpm test:e2e`. The real helper's host manifest goes into the Playwright user-data-dir (`employ-me install-test-host --real`), and `EMPLOY_ME_PROVIDER` is removed from the environment.
- **Backstop:** every request that isn't a GET or HEAD is aborted, on every host, and recorded. The one exception is Ashby's GraphQL *queries* (`ApiJobPosting`, `ApiOrganizationFromHostedJobsPageName`, `ApiAutocompleteGeoLocation`), because Ashby renders its form from them. A request body that holds a mutation is aborted (`e2e/live/backstop.test.ts`).
- **Budget:** a ledger counts real calls per CLI from the test host's log. A test that could take a CLI past 15 is skipped, and a test that goes past its own cap is stopped.
- **Persona:** Riley Gagnon, `riley.gagnon@example.com`, `+1 604 555 0187`, 1200 Example Ave, Vancouver BC. Canadian citizen. Authorized in Canada with no sponsorship. In the US: not authorized, needs sponsorship, not a "U.S. person". Two invented employers, UBC BASc. Salary CAD 150–175k and USD 170–200k. Every Self-ID answer declined.
- **Setup** went through the dashboard's setup flow. A synthetic Resume PDF (printed by Chromium from `e2e/live/persona.ts`) was read by the Provider, the review step was corrected by hand, and the questionnaire was answered. Every Fill then restored that Profile from a backup.
- **Passes:** first a rules-only pass on every Posting (no helper, no calls), to find rule bugs for free. Then one Provider pass per Posting, split between Claude and Codex. A final rules-only pass ran after the fixes, rebased on main at 3822ab6.

## Bugs found and fixed

Each fix has a test that failed on the behaviour seen live before the fix.

1. **An acknowledgement was ticked** (silently wrong, hard rule). 1Password's "I understand that offers of employment are conditional on … a background check." was ticked from the Profile's background-check answer. The willingness guard ("Will you consent to a background check?" is a fact) also screened out first-person statements. *core* `taxonomy/sensitive.ts`: the guard now applies to questions only. A "you can unsubscribe" sign-up is now consent too. Tests: `core/src/taxonomy/sensitive.test.ts`, `fill/src/classify/classify.test.ts`, fixture `ashby/1password-2026-10-28`.
2. **"No" typed as a referrer's name** (silently wrong). Wave: "If you were referred …, please provide the name of the person who referred you." *fill* `answer/background.ts`: a box asking for the referrer's name is Couldn't fill (notInProfile) with a fix. "Have you been referred …? If so, who?" stays a yes/no question. Test: `plan.test.ts`.
3. **A salary range where a number was asked** (silently wrong). Wave's "Please write down a number" box got "150,000 - 175,000 CAD per year". 1Password's "beginning" and "end of your desired range (no commas)" boxes would have got the whole range too; there they only went to review because the country was unclear. *fill* `answer/country.ts`: such a box gets that one number, and plain digits when asked. Test: `plan.test.ts`.
4. **Half a name** (silently wrong). Zoox: "What is your preferred first name and last name?" got "Riley". *fill* `answer/facts.ts`: it now gets first and last. Test: `plan.test.ts`.
5. **Ashby select-all-that-apply questions came out as one field per option**, with option text as the field id. Notion's "How did you hear about this opportunity?" became seven fields, six of them "Couldn't fill". 1Password's race question became twelve. *fill* new extract hook `checkboxGroup`, used by `adapters/ashby`; the field is keyed by the question's `data-field-path`. Tests: `extract.test.ts`, fixtures `ashby/1password` and `ashby/ramp`.
6. **Ramp's SMS and WhatsApp consent radios were both listed as "Phone".** *fill* `adapters/ashby`: they're named for what they ask. Test: `extract.test.ts`.
7. **A word in passing decided the type, and kept the question from the AI.** Ramp's "…You'll receive an email from CodeSignal" became an email field, and Palantir's "years of … post college work experience" became a school field. *fill* `classify/rules.ts`. Test: `classify.test.ts`.
8. **"Are you located in <country>?" wasn't answered.** Discord's US question found no option matching a city, and Wave's Canada question went to the AI. *fill* `answer/facts.ts` and `classify/rules.ts`: Yes or No from the address. Test: `plan.test.ts`.
9. **Any Work Authorization question the AI matched read as "country unclear".** Cohere misspells "sponsorship", so the rules missed the question and the AI matched it. It then went to review with both countries side by side, though the Posting is in Toronto. *fill* `classify/index.ts`: fields left to the AI carry the Posting's country. Re-checked live with Claude: "No", for review. Test: `plan.test.ts`.
10. **"Offices" (plural) wasn't recognised** in Notion's "We work from our offices on Mondays, Tuesdays and Thursdays". This affected both the rule and the answer. Re-checked with Codex: "Yes", for review. Test: `plan.test.ts`.
11. **Required questions only the AI recognised were never drafted.** On Ramp they ended as "Couldn't fill: The AI did not write a Draft", with no Draft button, though design.md drafts every required free-text question during the Fill.
    - *fill:* the AI's "needs a Draft" makes the field `freeText.question`, and it waits for its Draft in the next round. `ProviderFeedback` gains `draftsFailed`, so a Draft the Provider couldn't write stops the wait and shows its fix.
    - *extension side panel* (`sidepanel/fill-flow.ts`, `messaging/content.ts`, the interface only): the panel sends the failed ids.
    - Re-checked live: Claude drafted both of Ramp's questions, and Codex drafted one and showed Retry for the other.
    - Tests: `plan.test.ts`, `controller.test.ts`.
12. **Codex left the degree level empty for "BASc".** The *helper prompt* (`prompts/parse-resume.ts`) now says the usual abbreviations make the level clear. Test: `prompts.test.ts`. Not re-run live, to save calls.

## Left

**In fill (not fixed here):**
- A Self-ID question on a Posting whose country is unclear goes to review, even when every country's answer is "decline" (1Password: four rows).
- Export-control questions end as "No option matches your Profile". On Databricks the right choice for a non-US person is "none of the above". On Zoox it's a text box asking for countries of citizenship, so the reason wording is also wrong there.
- Years of experience has no field type, so the AI says unknown (Palantir, Ramp).
- "How did you hear" with no website option (Zoox) could pick "Other". "Are you a former 1Password employee?" isn't caught by the rules; the AI catches it.
- Ramp's "based in the U.S., Canada or LATAM, and/or authorized to work in one of them" goes to review as country unclear; it's Yes for either country.
- **"Filled" for a Resume means it was attached, not that it uploaded.** The backstop aborted every upload (Greenhouse's S3 POST, Ashby's upload handle, Lever's parse), so a live pass can't check that part. Greenhouse's widget stayed at "uploading" while the panel said Filled.

**Outside my area** (reported, not changed):
- *Extension, debug report:* it over-scrubs. Persona words such as "CAD", "Yes" and "Prefer not to say" are replaced inside field ids and labels (`cards[…f563[removed]9]`), so the report's ids no longer match the form.
- *Extension, side panel wording:*
  - For a company the Profile has no "worked here before" answer for, the panel says "You asked to answer this one each time."
  - A Draft that failed shows "The AI is unavailable", though the AI answered the field mapping.
- *Helper, Codex Provider:* one of two concurrent `draftAnswer` turns failed on Codex's side (`responseTooManyFailedAttempts`). One retry in the helper may be worth it.
