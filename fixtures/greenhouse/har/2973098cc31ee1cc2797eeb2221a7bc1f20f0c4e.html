LATAM/CANADA/EMEA/APAC/MEXICO Voluntary Demographic Questions

At MongoDB, we believe in fostering an environment where diverse perspectives can grow and thrive. We are committed to creating a culture of inclusion and a sense of belonging by seeking and valuing employees from different backgrounds. One of our core values as a company is to Embrace the Power of Differences.

In the spirit of inclusion, we invite candidates to self-identify as to their gender.  The information collected through this voluntary demographic survey is used in aggregate only.  Candidate responses to this question will help us to identify areas for improvement in our recruiting processes. If you choose to self-identify, your response will not be used in any hiring or subsequent employment decisions. You are not required to provide this information and you may select "I don't wish to answer". Declining to provide this information will not subject you to adverse treatment or retaliation.

All of your personal information, including your gender, should you choose to provide it, will be collected, stored and processed in accordance with MongoDB’s Privacy Notice. Please see our Privacy Notice here.
